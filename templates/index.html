    Notes from TripGenius

      Why TripGenius exists

      Planning shouldn't take longer than the trip.

      01

      Comparing hotels, routes, and costs across tabs eats an entire evening.

      02

      Manual day-by-day budgeting is error-prone and easy to get badly wrong.

      03

      Overwhelmed travellers postpone the trip entirely — or overspend on it.

      How it works

      One flow, three steps.

      1

      Tell the app

      Budget, number of days, destination — and, if you like, a travel style.

      2

      The engine plans instantly

      A trained cost model and a curated recommender build your itinerary, hotel tier, and budget split in seconds.

      3

      Book or share

      Save the plan as a PDF, or hand it straight to the group chat.

      On the roadmap

      Weather alerts

      Travel advisories

      Price predictions

      A nudge when flight or hotel prices drop, with a recommended booking window.
